I'm a Computer Science student at Ashesi University, Class of 2029. Most of my time right now goes into CADDE Internships, a platform I started that connects Ghanaian university students with SMEs looking for interns. I'm the founder and I write most of the code myself.

CADDE is live at cadde.me and as an iOS app. Students upload a CV, apply to listings with one tap, and get an AI match score that tells them how well they fit and what to improve. Employers get vetted by me before they can post, then see applicants ranked automatically instead of picking based on who they know. It's built on Node.js, Express, Supabase, the Claude API for CV scoring, and Paystack for payments.

Outside of CADDE I do freelance mobile and web development on Upwork. Before that I was a Python Developer intern at Codveda Technologies.
